Account and privacy

Delete your account

You can request deletion here without reinstalling BuddysCo. Sign in to your existing account, review what happens, then confirm.

What deletion does

  • Removes your active account, profile, private tasks and drafts, assistant access, and identifiable uploads owned by your account.
  • Removes your membership and personal contribution data from shared spaces. Your authored notes, quizzes and templates are removed. Other members’ groups, work and progress remain; your messages become content-free placeholders.
  • Does not delete your Google or Zoom account or provider-hosted meeting. Manage those with the provider.

Some anonymized shared records, safety reports, aggregate totals and security receipts can remain. Backup, storage soft-delete and service-log retention are separate from active app deletion. Older uploads without reliable ownership may need a verified support request. See the full deletion details.

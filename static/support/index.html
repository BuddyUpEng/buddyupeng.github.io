We can help

BuddysCo support

Email admin@buddysco.com for help with your account, study groups, safety concerns or privacy requests.

Delete your account

Use the secure account-deletion page to sign in and confirm removal without reinstalling the app. If you cannot sign in, email us with the subject “BuddysCo account deletion”. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you.

Never send your password, SMS verification code, identity documents or access tokens by email. Describe the issue and include only the information needed to identify your request.

Sign-in and app problems

Tell us your device model, operating-system version, BuddysCo version, and what you were trying to do. You can include a screenshot after removing private messages, contact details, verification codes and tokens. If sign-in is unavailable, do not create another account to recover the old one.

Meeting rooms

Group meeting links open Google Meet or Zoom. Provider admission rules, account limits and room expiry still apply. Ask the group creator to renew or replace a room when needed. BuddysCo account deletion does not delete a Google or Zoom account or a meeting hosted by those providers.

Safety and privacy

Read our community guidelines for respectful participation and reporting.

Use the app’s report and block controls where available, or email us about abusive content or account access concerns. Avoid forwarding private conversations unnecessarily. For emergencies or an immediate threat to safety, contact your local emergency services.

Read the privacy policy for information about shared records, account deletion, assistant access and provider services.

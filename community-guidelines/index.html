Community guidelines

BuddysCo is a place to learn with other people. Treat members respectfully and keep shared spaces useful for studying.

Keep the community safe

  • Do not harass, bully, threaten or post hateful content about other people.
  • Do not share sexual content, child exploitation material, or illegal content or graphic violence.
  • Do not publish another person’s private information without consent.
  • Do not impersonate others, spam, scam, or share content that infringes someone else’s rights.

Report and block

Report a concern from the group menu, through Profile → Report Problem, or by emailing admin@buddysco.com. Include only the information needed to explain the concern; never send passwords or verification codes.

Blocking prevents private contact, discovery and invitations. Existing shared groups remain until you leave them. Use the group’s leave control if you no longer want to share that space.

Violations may result in content removal or account restrictions. This page does not promise automated screening or a particular response time. For an immediate threat to safety, contact your local emergency services.
